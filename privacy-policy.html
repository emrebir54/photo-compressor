Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Compress - Photos & Videos ("the App") is provided by EEB Consultancy LLC ("we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information.

Photos and Videos

The App allows users to compress photos, resize images, and compress videos.

Photo and video processing takes place locally on your device. We do not upload, collect, store on our servers, sell, or share your photos or videos.

The App accesses photos and videos only with the permissions or selections you provide. Processed files are saved or exported when you request it.

  • Advertising-related data.
  • Crash reports, performance information, and other diagnostic data.

Some information may be processed by third-party service providers. Depending on the provider and configuration, certain data may be linked to your identity or used for tracking as defined by Apple's privacy requirements.

Your photos and videos remain on your device unless you choose to share or export them using features provided by your device.

Technical information processed by third-party services is subject to their applicable privacy and security practices.

Subscriptions and Purchases
